A BA degree offers diverse career paths ranging from education and journalism to corporate communication and government sector preparation.

Where Students May Work

  • Schools and Educational Institutions
  • Media houses, Newspapers, and Digital Platforms
  • Publishing Companies
  • Corporate Communication Departments
  • Government Sector (UPSC, GPSC prep)
