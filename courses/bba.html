Course Overview

About the Course

BBA is meant for students who want to understand how organizations work through subjects like management, marketing, finance, communication, and basic analytics. The course mixes theory with presentations, case-based discussion, and practical classroom activities.

Future Path

After the Course

After BBA, students often start with entry-level roles in sales, marketing support, operations, administration, or customer handling. Many students also continue with MBA or other postgraduate programs after building a clear base in business studies.

Common Starting Roles

  • • Sales Executive
  • • Marketing Coordinator
  • • HR Assistant
  • • Operations Executive
  • • Customer Relationship Executive

Where Students May Work

  • • Retail and e-commerce businesses
  • • Banking and service companies
  • • Small and medium enterprises
  • • Startup teams
  • • Office administration and support departments

Career growth in this field usually improves with communication skills, internship exposure, and practical workplace understanding.
