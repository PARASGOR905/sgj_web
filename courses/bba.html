About the Course

Future Path

After the Course

A BBA degree provides a recognized qualification for entry-level and junior executive roles across diverse industry sectors. It also serves as an excellent foundation for pursuing an MBA or starting one's own venture.

Common Starting Roles

  • Management Trainee
  • Marketing or Sales Executive
  • HR Assistant / Recruiter
  • Business Development Executive
  • Financial Analyst or Operations Assistant

Where Students May Work

  • Banks and Financial Institutions
  • Corporations and MNCs
  • Marketing and PR Agencies
  • Retail and E-commerce Companies
  • Startups and their own entrepreneurial ventures
